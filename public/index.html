Purpose-built for visible Gemini watermarks — not a generic magic eraser.

MBLabs combines known Gemini geometry, alpha inversion, and local cleanup models. It is deliberately scoped, predictable, and inspectable.

Known geometry first

Detection starts from validated Gemini/Veo output catalogs and local anchor refinement instead of guessing a model outline.

Inverse alpha, not hallucination

Supported marks are removed by reversing the compositing equation. Content is not invented or “filled in” by a remote model.

Local by design

Processing happens on your computer through the browser, CLI, or packaged tool. Media is not uploaded to a hosted processor.
